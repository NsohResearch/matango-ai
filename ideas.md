<idea>
  **Design Movement**: Cyber-Noir / Neo-Brutalism Fusion
  **Core Principles**:
  1. **High Contrast & Impact**: Bold typography against deep, dark backgrounds to create a sense of exclusivity and power.
  2. **Raw & Digital**: Embracing the "artificial" nature of AI with glitch effects, monospaced fonts, and exposed grid lines.
  3. **Immersive Depth**: Using heavy shadows, neon glows, and layered elements to create a 3D-like environment.
  4. **Direct & Unapologetic**: Clear, punchy copy and large, confident imagery.

  **Color Philosophy**:
  - **Primary**: Electric Lime (#CCFF00) - Represents energy, digital novelty, and stands out aggressively against the dark.
  - **Secondary**: Deep Violet (#7C3AED) - Adds a layer of mystery and luxury, balancing the harshness of the lime.
  - **Background**: Void Black (#050505) - Not just dark gray, but deep black to make colors pop.
  - **Intent**: To evoke a feeling of "hacking the system" or accessing a secret, powerful tool.

  **Layout Paradigm**:
  - **Asymmetric Grid**: Breaking the standard 12-column grid with overlapping elements and floating cards.
  - **Split-Screen Hero**: A vertical divide in the hero section, with text on one side and a dynamic, floating 3D element or character on the other.
  - **Horizontal Scroll**: Utilizing horizontal scrolling for influencer discovery to mimic a "gallery" or "selection screen" feel.

  **Signature Elements**:
  - **Neon Borders**: Thin, glowing borders on active elements.
  - **Glitch Text**: Subtle hover effects on headings that distort or shift channels.
  - **Floating Glass Cards**: Semi-transparent, blurred backgrounds for influencer profiles to separate them from the void.

  **Interaction Philosophy**:
  - **Snap & Hover**: Elements should snap into place, and hover states should be instant and dramatic (e.g., color inversion).
  - **Micro-Interactions**: Buttons that "press" down, toggles that spark, and loading states that look like code compiling.

  **Animation**:
  - **Entrance**: Elements slide in from different directions with a "staggered" effect.
  - **Scroll**: Parallax effects on background elements (grids, floating shapes).
  - **Hover**: "Glitch" or "scanline" effects on images.

  **Typography System**:
  - **Headings**: **Space Grotesk** (Bold/Display) - Technical yet quirky.
  - **Body**: **JetBrains Mono** or **Roboto Mono** - Reinforcing the "code/tech" aspect.
  - **Hierarchy**: Massive, uppercase headings paired with small, technical metadata labels.
</idea>

  - **Secondary**: Holographic Silver - For accents and borders.
  - **Background**: Deep Midnight Blue (#0F172A) to Soft Purple gradients.
  - **Intent**: To make the technology feel magical, accessible, and premium.

  **Layout Paradigm**:
  - **Centralized Focus with Floating Elements**: Key content is centered but surrounded by floating, blurred orbs or shapes.
  - **Masonry Grid**: For influencer discovery, creating a more organic, "Pinterest-like" exploration experience.
  - **Overlay Navigation**: A floating dock or blurred top bar that doesn't obstruct the content.

  **Signature Elements**:
  - **Frosted Glass**: Heavy use of backdrop-filter: blur() on cards and modals.
  - **Soft Glows**: Elements emit a gentle light rather than having hard shadows.
  - **Gradient Text**: Headings that flow between colors.

  **Interaction Philosophy**:
  - **Flow**: Interactions should feel like moving through water—smooth, damped, and natural.
  - **Soft Feedback**: Gentle pulses or glows instead of sharp color changes.

  **Animation**:
  - **Float**: Background elements gently bob and weave.
  - **Fade & Scale**: Content fades in and scales up slightly on scroll.
  - **Morph**: Buttons that morph into loading spinners or success states.

  **Typography System**:
  - **Headings**: **Outfit** or **Plus Jakarta Sans** - Geometric but friendly.
  - **Body**: **Inter** or **Satoshi** - Clean, legible, and neutral.
  - **Hierarchy**: Large, thin headings paired with medium-weight body text.

<idea>
  **Design Movement**: Editorial Vogue / High-Fashion Tech
  **Core Principles**:
  1. **Image-First**: The interface serves as a frame for the high-quality AI imagery.
  2. **Bold Typography**: Using typography as a graphic element, often overlapping images.
  3. **Structured Elegance**: Rigid, magazine-style layouts with clear lines and dividers.
  4. **Monochrome with a Twist**: Black and white base with a single, striking accent color.

  **Color Philosophy**:
  - **Primary**: Acid Green (#BFFF00) or Hot Pink (#FF00FF) - Used sparingly for CTAs and accents.
  - **Base**: Stark White (#FFFFFF) and Pitch Black (#000000).
  - **Intent**: To position the platform as a high-end fashion magazine or agency portfolio.

  **Layout Paradigm**:
  - **Split-Screen & Overlap**: Text overlapping images, images overlapping text.
  - **Sticky Scrolling**: Sections that stick while content scrolls over them.
  - **Magazine Grid**: Varied column widths and image aspect ratios.

  **Signature Elements**:
  - **Thin Lines**: Hairline dividers between sections and grid items.
  - **Large Serifs**: Using a high-contrast serif font for headings.
  - **Marquee Text**: Scrolling text banners for announcements or trending tags.

  **Interaction Philosophy**:
  - **Precise**: Sharp, instant hover states.
  - **Cursor Effects**: Custom cursor that changes based on the element being hovered (e.g., "View" text appears).

  **Animation**:
  - **Reveal**: Images reveal with a "curtain" effect.
  - **Parallax**: Text and images scroll at different speeds.
  - **Typewriter**: Text appearing character by character.

  **Typography System**:
  - **Headings**: **Playfair Display** or **Cinzel** - Elegant, high-contrast serif.
  - **Body**: **Manrope** or **DM Sans** - Modern, geometric sans-serif.
  - **Hierarchy**: Huge, dramatic headlines with small, tracked-out captions.
</idea>
