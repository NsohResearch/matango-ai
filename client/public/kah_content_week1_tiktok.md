## Posting Times (Optimal for TikTok)

- **Morning:** 7:00 AM (catching early scrollers)
- **Lunch:** 12:00 PM (lunch break browsers)
- **Evening:** 7:00 PM (after work wind-down)
- **Night:** 10:00 PM (late night scrollers)

Rotate between these times and track which performs best.

## Engagement Strategy

**First Hour After Posting:**
- Reply to every comment within 60 minutes
- Ask follow-up questions to boost engagement
- Pin the best/most relevant comment

**Daily:**
- Engage with 10-20 posts in #marketingtiktok
- Duet/stitch relevant marketing content
- Follow accounts in target audience
